GATHERUP - UPDATED PATCH (email verification removed)
=======================================================

You said you couldn't even get into the dashboard, so email verification has
been removed completely. Here's exactly what changed:

1. backend/routers/auth.py
   - /auth/register no longer sends a verification email or creates an
     EmailVerification record. It just creates the account.
   - /auth/login no longer checks email verification status at all. Any
     existing account (verified or not) and any new account can log in
     immediately with just email + password.

2. frontend/js/app.js
   - After registering, you're sent straight to /login instead of a
     "check your email" page.
   - Login no longer has special-case handling for a "verify your email"
     error, since that error can no longer happen.

3. frontend/dashboard.html
   - Removed the "Email address verified / not verified" banner from the
     Account modal, since there's nothing to verify anymore.

PLUS the earlier fixes from the previous patch are still included:
   - backend/main.py: creates the missing polls/poll_options/poll_votes
     tables on startup (this is why polls were completely broken before).
   - frontend/dashboard.html + group.html: fixed the missing "Chat" route
     so the sidebar/quick-action Chat link actually opens chat.
   - frontend/dashboard.html: browser now actually asks for notification
     permission, so desktop chat popups can fire.

IMPORTANT - your existing accounts
-----------------------------------
If you already have accounts stuck as "unverified" in your database, you
don't need to do anything else - the login check is gone, so they'll log
in immediately now.

INSTALL
-------
1. Stop Uvicorn.
2. Copy these files into your project, overwriting the old versions:
     backend/main.py
     backend/routers/auth.py
     frontend/dashboard.html
     frontend/group.html
     frontend/js/app.js
3. Restart: uvicorn backend.main:app --reload
4. Hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R).

You can ignore the SMTP_* settings in .env.example entirely now - they're
no longer used for anything.
